The watershed boundary files live here.

Four watersheds have been delineated using the USGS StreamStats tool: https://streamstats.usgs.gov/ss/
The first three are delineated to USGS stream gauge sites:
  12090400.geojson
  12090500.geojson
  12091500.geojson

The fourth is delineated to a point just before Clover Creek joins the North Fork of Clover Creek at Latitude: 47.13382907685807, Longitude: -122.42997408965459. I manually selected this point along a streamline.
  ungauged.geojson
This watershed area does not have a USGS streamgauge, but encompasses the area of interest for our community partners.

We also have the flowlines, NHDFlowline.shp, the actual location of streams, from the National Hydrography Dataset, but downloaded just for Washington from https://geo.wa.gov/datasets/71fa52e7d6224fde8b09facb12b30f04_3/about.
